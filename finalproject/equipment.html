Equipment Guide

Modern pickleball equipment has evolved rapidly. Understanding paddle generations, shapes, and playing characteristics will help you make informed equipment decisions and improve your game.

Paddle Generations

Gen 1

Traditional honeycomb-core paddles focused on consistency and control.

Gen 2

Thermoformed paddles that increased stability, spin potential, and power.

Gen 3 & Gen 4

Advanced foam technologies and innovative core designs focused on expanding performance.
