Courts & Strategy

Strategy is often the difference between winning and losing. Understanding positioning, shot selection, and teamwork can dramatically improve your performance.

Essential Pickleball Strategies

Get To The Kitchen

The non-volley zone line is generally the strongest position on the court. Moving forward quickly after quality drops or drives helps maintain offensive pressure.

Attack High Balls

Balls above net height are opportunities to speed up play and force weak returns from your opponents.

Work As A Team

Doubles partners should move together and avoid leaving gaps that opponents can exploit.
