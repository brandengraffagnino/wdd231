What Is Pickleball?

Pickleball is one of the fastest-growing sports in the United States. Combining elements of tennis, badminton, and table tennis, it offers a fun and competitive experience for players of all ages and skill levels.

Whether you are just starting out or looking to take your game to the next level, understanding equipment and strategy can make a significant difference.

A Brief History

Pickleball was created in 1965 and has grown from a backyard game into an internationally recognized sport. Today, millions of players enjoy pickleball at recreation centers, dedicated facilities, parks, and tournaments throughout the world.
